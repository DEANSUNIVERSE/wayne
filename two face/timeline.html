GOTHAM CITY LORE TIMELINE

A chronological sequence of major tactical crises, rogue births, and Gotham City transformations recorded in the Batcomputer mainframes.

YEAR ONE // ORIGIN

THE BAT IS BORN

After a decade of global combat training, Bruce Wayne returns to a corrupt Gotham City. The Bat-Signal is illuminated for the first time, establishing a symbol of fear for crime.

YEAR THREE // RISE OF ROGUES

THE LONG HALLOWEEN & TWO-FACE

Traditional mob families collapse under the Holiday Serial Killer case. District Attorney Harvey Dent suffers an acid attack, giving birth to Two-Face and shifting Gotham into theatrical super-villainy.

YEAR FIVE // TRAGEDY

A DEATH IN THE FAMILY

The Joker executes a brutal ambush on Jason Todd (Robin II). The Dark Knight suffers his darkest loss, driving him into colder, harsher isolation.

YEAR SEVEN // SIEGE

KNIGHTFALL CRISIS

Bane orchestrates a mass Arkham Asylum breakout to exhaust Batman physically and mentally before confronting him in the Batcave and breaking the Bat.

YEAR NINE // ISOLATION

NO MAN'S LAND

A cataclysmic earthquake strikes Gotham, causing the US government to declare the city uninhabitable and destroy all access bridges. Batman fights gang wars to rebuild order block by block.

PRESENT DAY // SHADOWS

THE COURT OF OWLS

Batman uncovers Gotham's centuries-old secret society that has ruled from the city's hidden architectures using undead assassin Talons.

CURRENT // PROTOCOL 10

ARKHAM CITY LOCKDOWN

Gotham's slums are converted into a walled mega-prison. Batcomputer shifts into level 10 readiness mode to safeguard city borders.
